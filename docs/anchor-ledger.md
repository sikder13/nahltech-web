# Anchor-phrase ledger

Anchor phrases already spent on internal links, one line per target URL,
alphabetical by target. Before linking a page listed here, choose a phrase not
already on its line. Whenever content ships with an internal link, add its
phrase to the target's line (or add the line) in the same commit.

Counted: every anchor someone chose the words for, in page copy, posts and
research, including a card whose heading is an approved anchor. Not counted:
header, footer and nav links, and card or list headings that repeat the
target's own title; those repeat by design, and `npm run crawl:check` exempts
them too. Phrases are exact, case included.

Seeded 13 September 2026 from a crawl of the built site. Targets without a line
have not been recorded yet; that does not mean nothing links them.
