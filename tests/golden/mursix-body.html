The squeeze behind the number

The squeeze came from both ends of your business this year: fewer vehicles to make parts for, and more expensive metal to make them from BENCHMARK.

  • Your largest market sold 5.8 percent fewer vehicles in August than a year earlier BENCHMARK. Electric vehicles fell to 7.7 percent of sales, from 11.9 percent a year earlier BENCHMARK.
  • Since April 6, 2026, Section 232 duties apply to the full customs value of covered imports, and parts of stamping dies are listed on the annex BENCHMARK.

What you have already built

Your presses already report through SmartPAC, and your team built Murray Mentor to keep the floor's knowledge OBSERVED. This is the layer neither was built for: what the metal itself does to your margin, in dollars, by part.

What we propose

The proposal is deliberately small. In three to four weeks, for a fixed fee between $5,000 and $7,500, we would split your top part numbers into metal value and fabrication value against the public curves, map your reclaim stream from your settlement statements, and hand you the measured number. Read-only; nothing touches production. If your records cannot support the work, we say so in the first week and deliver a data-readiness report at the same price.

What you receive

Metal value and fabrication value, by part
Your top part numbers split into the metal each one carries, valued at the dated public curves, and the fabrication value left in the quoted price, with how far each margin moves for every 10 percent move in metal.
Your reclaim stream, mapped
Shipped scrap weight and contained metal, priced at spot on the ship date, reconciled against what your refiner settlements paid, with the difference itemized into the non-payable share, fees, float and anything unexplained.
The measured number
Your annual metal margin exposure, measured from your own records in place of our range, and shown against the steel, silver and vehicle series.

How the four weeks run

  3. Week fourA 45-minute readout with you and anyone you choose. One page of findings, the workbook behind it, and everything handed over, your data included.
