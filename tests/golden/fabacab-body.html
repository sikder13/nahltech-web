What we propose

The proposal is deliberately small. In two to three weeks, for a fixed fee between $1,500 and $2,500 settled in one call, we would measure four numbers from your records: the remake rate and its cost by cause, what your quotes absorbed, where time goes inside your lead time (qCab publishes four weeks or less, SnapCab five to six express, your site eight to ten after approvals), and the modernization share of your order book. Read-only; nothing installed, nothing changed.

What you receive

The remake rate, by cause
Every rebuilt job in your records, counted and costed, split into field measurement, submittal misread, freight damage and shop error, with every classification confirmed by your people before it counts.
What your quotes absorbed
Each job's materials priced at quote date and again at purchase date against the public index, so you see the drift a fixed, all-inclusive price ate. If you buy the day you quote, this measurement returns zero and we say so in writing.
Where time goes inside your lead time
Your eight to ten weeks split into working time and waiting time as far as your records allow, set beside the published lead times above.
The modernization share of your order book
Your orders split between modernization and new construction, by quarter, set against the Otis and Census series.

How the two to three weeks run
