What we propose

The proposal is deliberately small. In three to four weeks, for a fixed fee between $5,000 and $7,500, we would measure four numbers from your records: the rework rate and its cost by cause, what your fixed quotes absorbed, waiting time against your published working time at each approval, and the launch share of your order book. Read-only; we work from exports, and nothing touches your quality system.

What you receive

Rework rate and cost, by cause
From your quality and job records, trailing twelve months: how many trays came back or were redone, and what each cost, sorted into four buckets: coating guarantee honors, validation failures, customer mid-project instrument changes, and internal errors.
What your fixed quotes absorbed
For programs quoted at a fixed price: the aluminum, stainless and coating content of the quote priced at quote date against the same content priced at delivery date, using the federal series. The result is the dollar amount your quotes silently ate, or did not.
Waiting time against working time
The calendar days each project actually spent between layout start and production release, split at each customer approval, as far as your records allow, set against your published working days.
Launch share and quote-to-cash
What share of revenue rode on new product launches against sustaining and replacement orders, and the days from quote to cash for each.

How the three to four weeks run

  1. Week oneYour records arrive as exports and we build the inventory: what exists and what is missing, per measurement. The readiness verdict comes in writing either way.
  2. Weeks two to fourWe build the four measurements and deliver the calibrated model, the findings letter and the hand-over. You review before anything is final.
